The story

Emily's family keeps pushing her to find a boyfriend. To get them off her back she says she already has one. It works — until her sister's wedding comes around and everyone expects to meet him. The boyfriend does not exist.

So she asks Thijs to stand in and play the part for one wedding, so the lie holds. Her family believes the relationship. It is not real.

Two viewpoints

The game has two playable characters, Emily and Thijs, and the viewpoint moves between them inside a single story rather than splitting into separate routes. The first scene opens as Emily and hands over to Thijs partway through; the second is mostly Thijs with stretches as Emily, and so on.

Both default names can be changed by the player in the settings.

Choices branch the story. That branching is a separate thing from the viewpoint switching — the story is played through once, seen from both sides.
