Branch templates

Each one drops in a complete, already-wired pattern with unique label names and a converge point, so nothing is left dangling. Fill in the condition and the lines afterwards. Inserted after the selected command, or at the end.

Paste dialogue

One line per line of dialogue. Liisa: Hello. becomes a say with a speaker; a line with no Name: in front becomes narration. Start a line with -  to force narration (needed when the line itself contains a colon), and with // for a comment the tool throws away.

Lines starting with @ are commands: @label name, @jump name, @scene scene_0002, @bg bg/room.png, @sprite liisa sprites/liisa_1.png center, @remove liisa, @set panic add 6, @style textbox colorful_textbox, @wait 500, @end. Choices and conditional jumps are not in the shorthand — build those in the form, where the label lists and condition editors are.
